A. HERO / POND SECTION
This is still the emotional anchor of the site and needs the biggest art-direction upgrade.

Current problems:
- it reads as “generated image + some fish copies” rather than a living painted pond
- water surface is not convincing
- reflections and depth are too weak
- fish motion feels inherited from the earlier experiment rather than authored for this site
- letters vanish too much
- the overall painterly / anime-like pond vision is not fully there

Target direction:
- overhead or near-overhead Persian courtyard pond / howz with painterly, anime-adjacent, hand-painted sensibility
- visible water depth and surface language
- turquoise / firooze tile influence, floral / lotus motifs, pond-edge / step cues where appropriate
- botanical life: lotus, lily pads, small plant accents, but not cluttered
- subtle light play in the water: caustics, ripples, reflection shimmer, reflected brightness near lower areas
- 2 or 3 fish only, not many
- everything should feel calm, elegant, and alive

Required fixes:
1. Rebuild the pond visual system, not just by tweaking one background image.
   The section should feel layered:
   - pond base / bottom art
   - water coloration/depth layer
   - reflection / caustic layer
   - fish layer
   - floating letters layer
   - subtle plant layer
2. Fish must no longer be perceived as simple copies of a previous experiment.
   - Use better-authored fish visuals.
   - Distinct individuals, even if they share a family look.
   - Top-down view must make visual sense.
   - Motion should feel authored for the pond.
3. The letters should fall as individual parchment pieces.
   - Each letter on its own small paper.
   - Motion must feel physical and distinct.
   - Some papers can sink more, some stay afloat, some drift slowly.
   - They remain in the pond after the intro.
4. Intro behavior:
   - page starts locked
   - the name sequence happens clearly
   - letters hit water and become part of the pond
   - scrolling unlocks only after the sequence completes
   - replay on refresh / revisit at top should work reliably
5. Remove any feeling that the pond is just a static decorative wallpaper.
   It should feel like an environment.
